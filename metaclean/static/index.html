Five things a photo or video can say about you

Photo metadata is information saved inside the file next to the picture. Phones and apps add it automatically, and it travels with the photo when you share it. This is part of what one ordinary phone photo carries, printed the way a forensics tool shows it:

  1. 1

    Where it was taken

    Exact position, often accurate to a few metres. Enough to find a home or a school.

  2. 2

    What it was taken with

    Phone or camera model, lens and serial number. A serial number links every photo from one device.

  3. 3

    Who took it and when

    Your name, the device's name, and the date, time and time zone of every shot.

  4. 4

    AI labels

    Content Credentials (C2PA), IPTC source types and AI prompts. Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok read them.

  5. 5

    Hidden copies

    Thumbnails and motion-photo video saved inside the file can show what you cropped out.

Other ways to remove photo details

Take a screenshot
It drops the details, but you lose resolution, and your phone writes a fresh set of its own: its model and the time of the screenshot.
Re-save it in an editor
Some editors keep the location. Most re-compress the picture and add their own name to the file.
Upload it and trust the platform
Most social sites hide the location from other people, but they receive your original first, and they read any AI label before they remove it.

Questions people ask

What is UnMeta?

UnMeta is a free, open-source website that removes hidden metadata from photos, videos, audio and documents: GPS location, phone model and serial numbers, names, dates and AI labels such as C2PA Content Credentials. It works entirely in your browser, so files are never uploaded, and it never re-encodes the picture or sound. There is no account, no ads and no daily limit.

What is photo metadata?

Photo metadata is information saved inside a photo file alongside the picture: where and when it was taken, the camera or phone used, its serial number, the author, editing history and, increasingly, labels saying whether AI made or edited it. It travels with the photo when you share it, unless the app or website removes it.

What does UnMeta remove from my photos?

GPS location and place names, camera and phone model, serial numbers and device names, author names and dates, editing history, AI labels (C2PA Content Credentials, IPTC Digital Source Type and AI generator prompts) and hidden copies such as thumbnails and motion-photo video.

Does removing photo metadata reduce quality?

No. UnMeta never re-compresses a photo. It removes the hidden details around the picture and checks that the compressed picture data is byte-for-byte identical to the original.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. UnMeta reads and cleans photos and videos inside your web browser, so they never leave your device. They're read only while the page is open and forgotten when you start over or close it.

Can I check that my photos aren't uploaded?

Yes. Turn off your Wi-Fi or unplug your network, then clean a photo. It still works, because everything happens inside your browser. The page is also built so the browser blocks it from sending data anywhere: it can't make uploads of any kind.

Does UnMeta work offline?

Yes. After your first visit, UnMeta is saved in your browser, so it opens and cleans photos and videos with no internet connection.

Isn't a screenshot enough to remove photo details?

A screenshot drops the original details, but it lowers quality, crops to your screen and adds new details of its own, such as your device and the time you took the screenshot. UnMeta removes the details and keeps the original picture exactly as it was.

Can UnMeta remove invisible AI watermarks like SynthID?

No. Invisible watermarks such as Google SynthID are part of the picture itself, not details stored around it, so no metadata tool can remove them. Anything visible in the photo, like faces or street signs, also stays.

Do I still need to say an image was made with AI?

Removing an AI label doesn't change how an image was made. Many platforms, and some laws, ask you to disclose AI-generated or AI-edited content. Check the rules that apply to you before you post.

Does UnMeta work with videos and voice recordings?

Yes. It removes the location, phone model, recording date, names and AI labels saved in MP4 and MOV videos and in M4A, MP3 and WAV recordings. The video and sound are copied exactly; only the details around them change. Videos can be up to 4 GB.

Which file formats does UnMeta support?

Photos: JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC and HEIF, AVIF, TIFF, and RAW formats including DNG, CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, ORF, RW2 and RAF. Videos: MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP. Audio: M4A voice memos, MP3 and WAV. Documents: PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint.

What UnMeta can and can't do

  • Never leaves your browser

    Photos and videos are read and cleaned inside this page and never uploaded. They're read only while the page is open and forgotten when you start over or close it.

  • You can check it yourself

    Turn off your Wi-Fi now and clean a photo: it still works. After your first visit it even opens offline. The browser also blocks this page from uploading anything.

  • Removes hidden details

    Location, device and serial numbers, names, dates, AI labels and hidden copies stored inside the file.

  • Can't change what's in the picture

    Faces, street signs, screens and reflections stay visible, and voices stay audible. So do invisible watermarks woven into the pixels or sound, like Google's SynthID.

  • Removing an AI label doesn't change how something was made

    Many platforms ask you to say when content was made with AI. Check their rules before you post.

  • Your originals stay as they are

    You download clean copies. Originals change only if you choose to replace them when cleaning a folder.
